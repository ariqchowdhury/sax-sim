Observation

Alto saxophone, from first principles

Lungs → vocal tract → lips → reed → mouthpiece → bore & tone holes → bell. Grab anything in the 3D view: lips, tongue, jaw, lungs, keys, mouthpiece interior.

Hold Space to blow · play notes with the keyboard (Z…M, Q…U) · ? for help

Controls

Mouse

  • Keys: click = hold/release, Shift+click = momentary
  • Lower lip: drag along mouthpiece = take-in (lip position), toward reed = lip force
  • Upper lip: drag along = take-in, ↕ = lip damping
  • Jaw / chin: drag ↕ = jaw opening
  • Tongue body (pink handle): drag = front/back & low/high
  • Tongue tip (orange): ↕ = tip height; drop onto the reed = tonguing
  • Glottis (violet): ↕ = glottal opening
  • Lungs / gauge (blue): drag ↕ = lung pressure
  • Mouthpiece cutaway: tip opening, facing length, baffle, chamber, throat, insertion handles; reed ↕ = strength
  • Orbit: left-drag empty space · pan: right-drag · zoom: wheel

Keyboard

  • Space blow (attack/release envelope) · / tongue on reed
  • ` toggle Note ↔ Direct-key mode · Esc release all
  • Note mode: Z S X D C V G B H N J M = C4…B4, Q 2 W 3 E R 5 T 6 Y 7 U = C5…B5, I 9 O 0 P [ = ] ⌫ \ = C6…A6 (written; G6+ use the altissimo fingerings); ↑/↓ octave shift (↓ gives B♭3/B3 on J/M)
  • Direct keys: Q octave · A S D LH1-3 · G bis · J K L RH1-3 · W E R palm D/E♭/F · T front F · Z X C V LH pinky G♯/C♯/B/B♭ · ; ' RH pinky E♭/C · U I O side E/C/B♭ · P high F♯
  • MIDI (panel → MIDI & vibrato): notes finger + blow, CC2/CC11 breath, mod wheel = vibrato
  • ● Rec records a WAV; panel → Presets & export for CSV telemetry and preset files
  • Shift+1-4 camera · Shift+X X-ray · Shift+C cutaway · Shift+F air flow
