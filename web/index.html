Help & shortcuts

Grab the parts

  • Parts you can grab glow softly and show a tag (Layers → Grab hints); the cursor turns into a hand. While dragging, the card next to the cursor shows the value you are changing and what it does to the pitch.
  • Lungs ↕ = blowing pressure (while blowing it sets the blow target) · Tongue ↔ front/back, ↕ low/high · tongue tip ↕, drop it on the reed to tongue · jaw ↕ · glottis ↕
  • Lower lip: along the mouthpiece = take-in, toward the reed = lip force · upper lip: ↕ = firmness
  • Mouthpiece (cutaway): tip opening, facing, baffle, chamber, throat, cork position; reed ↕ = strength
  • Keys: click = hold / release, Shift+click = momentary · Esc releases all
  • Shift while dragging = fine adjustment · empty space: drag = orbit, right-drag = pan, wheel / pinch = zoom

Panels

  • Scopes Shift+S: oscilloscope, spectrum, standing wave, input impedance (+ tract overlay) and the pressure / flow numbers
  • Controls Shift+P: every parameter, presets (save / import / export), MIDI & vibrato, keyboard options, WAV / CSV recording
  • Coach: record your own playing and get it analysed

Play

  • Space or the Hold to blow button · / tongue on reed · ` Note ↔ Direct-key mode
  • Note mode: Z S X D C V G B H N J M = C4…B4, Q 2 W 3 E R 5 T 6 Y 7 U = C5…B5, I 9 O 0 P [ = ] ⌫ \ = C6…A6 (written; G6+ altissimo); ↑/↓ octave shift
  • Direct keys: Q octave · A S D LH1-3 · G bis · J K L RH1-3 · W E R palm D/E♭/F · T front F · Z X C V LH pinky G♯/C♯/B/B♭ · ; ' RH pinky E♭/C · U I O side E/C/B♭ · P high F♯
  • MIDI (Controls → MIDI & vibrato): notes finger + blow, CC2/CC11 breath, mod wheel = vibrato

Altissimo: the tract in series

The reed works against Zbore + Ztract. Above the normal range the bore resonances are weak, but a high, front tongue with a narrower glottis makes a strong tract resonance. Grab the tongue: the card shows the tract resonance (cyan) against the note; the airway turns green when it is tuned to it. Try preset Altissimo setup, then ↑ and T/6/Y (G6/G♯6/A6).
